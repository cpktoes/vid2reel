<objective>
Make HDR clips come out in natural ordinary colour (tone-mapped on the processor with zscale and tonemap, D-11, D-12, D-14), keep the clip if that conversion ever fails (D-13), and flag 120 and 240 fps slow-motion sources while keeping them at real speed (D-04, NORM-04).

Plain English for the user: every clip her iPhone records is HDR. Without this plan those clips would look grey and washed out, the first fault the guide's Watch-out box names. After it, HDR clips are converted to ordinary colour; if the conversion fails on a clip, she still gets the clip (with flat colour) and one sentence saying so. Slow-motion clips are marked as such and play at normal speed.

Purpose: HDR is the norm in her footage, so this is the most visible part of normalize.
Output: the HDR branch of `video_chain`, the D-13 retry in `normalize()`, colour and slow-motion notes, and their tests.
</objective>

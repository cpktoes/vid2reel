<objective>
Turn photos into still clips (NORM-03, D-08, D-09, D-10): Pillow and pillow-heif open her HEIC, JPEG and PNG photos, turn them upright, save the full-size picture as an upright JPEG in work/, and FFmpeg turns that JPEG into a still clip of `PHOTO_SECONDS` seconds.

Plain English for the user: her photos are HEIC, which the sandbox's FFmpeg cannot open. After this plan a photo she attaches becomes a 4-second still clip like any other clip (so later steps handle one kind of thing), and the full-size photo is kept beside it as C-photo.jpg so Stage 16's slow push-in has detail to work with. The copy carries no location or camera data.

Purpose: photos are part of almost every reel she makes.
Output: the photo branch of normalize.py and its tests.
</objective>

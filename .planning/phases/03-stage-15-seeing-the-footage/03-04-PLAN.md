Plain English for the user: after this plan, look.json says for every half second whether the camera is still, panning or shaky, whether something in the frame is moving (only judged while the camera is still), how sharp and how bright the picture is, how loud the sound is, and whether the shot changed; and for each clip its overall brightness and colour cast, which Stage 16 will use to make clips sit together. Each of these is proved on a test clip made with a known answer.

Purpose: the extra stills (plan 06) and the candidate moments (plan 07) are built from these numbers; Stage 16's colour matching reads `overall`.

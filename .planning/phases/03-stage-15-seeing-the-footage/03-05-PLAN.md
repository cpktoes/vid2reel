<objective>
Make the contact sheets behave across a whole run: short clips share sheets in letter order (D-15), each sheet keeps one orientation, odd-shaped stills fit their cells, the run's rhythm thins evenly before measuring when it would pass the cap of 20 sheets (D-16) and the cap always holds, and the sheets degrade gracefully (byte guard, no Pillow, no select filter, no scalable font, a full disk).

Plain English for the user: after this plan, ten short clips cost a handful of sheets rather than ten, because their stills run on from one clip to the next (the labels keep them apart). A sideways clip in a tall reel gets its own sheet of wide stills, so nothing is squashed. If a run would need more than 20 sheets, look takes a still every 1.5 or 2 seconds instead of every second and says so in one sentence. To see it: run look on a few short clips and open work/sheets/sheet-01.jpg; the labels change letter part-way across the grid.

Purpose: sheets are what the chat's Claude reads, and every sheet costs part of her plan's allowance (the guide's cap).
Output: v2r/look/sheets.py and pick.py grown, the up-front rhythm decision in v2r/look/__init__.py, tests/test_look_sheets.py.
</objective>

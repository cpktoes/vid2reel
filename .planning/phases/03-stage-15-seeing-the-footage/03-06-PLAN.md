<objective>
Add the extra stills that make the sheets catch what a one-a-second rhythm misses: at shot changes, at peaks of movement while the camera is still, and at peaks of loudness, each placed where the moment is, kept apart from other stills and capped at a third more than the base stills (D-01, D-17); and prove with known-answer clips that a still lands on the moment that matters (LOOK-06).

Plain English for the user: a splash or a jump that lasts a third of a second can fall between two once-a-second stills. After this plan, look adds a still exactly where the measurements say something happened: a new shot, a burst of movement, or a loud moment such as a laugh. On a sheet these stills have in-between labels such as "A 04.6". A 30-second clip gets at most 10 of them, the strongest first.

Purpose: the guide's whole point for this stage ("a still every second would miss a splash"), and the Watch out box's rule: add stills where the measurements say something happened, not everywhere.
Output: `peaks` and `extra_times` in v2r/look/pick.py, the wiring in v2r/look/__init__.py, `white_share` and a bursts clip in tests/media.py, tests/test_look_extras.py.
</objective>

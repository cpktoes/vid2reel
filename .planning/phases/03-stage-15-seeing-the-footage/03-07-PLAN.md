<objective>
Give each clip a short ranked list of candidate moments: two-second stretches that are steady and sharp, ordered by steadiness and then by movement, merged where they run together, each with the stills it rests on and a reason in plain facts (D-18), and make sure nothing in look.json ever reads as a choice (D-04).

Plain English for the user: after this plan, look.json lists for each clip up to five stretches worth a look, such as "04.0 to 06.0: steady camera, sharp, subject moving, sound peak at 04.5; stills A 04.0, A 04.5, A 05.0". These are facts offered to the chat's Claude, in an order; the code never says which one to use. Shaky or blurred stretches and stretches that cross a cut are never listed.

Purpose: the chat's Claude gets ready-made receipts for "which two seconds and why, citing the stills"; the guide's blind-test prompt asks exactly that.

   - `windows(seconds, fps)`: (start, end) pairs from 0 in steps of CANDIDATE_STEP whose end start + CANDIDATE_SECONDS does not pass the clip's end, compared in whole frames; a clip shorter than CANDIDATE_SECONDS gives one window (0, seconds).

     - A window covers the halves whose `t` lies in [start, end). It is left out when any of its halves has camera_state "shaky", any has sharp false, or a shot change lies strictly between start and end in whole frames (a cut exactly at either edge is allowed). Values that are None (not measured) never exclude a window.
     - Tier: 1 when every half's camera_state is "still"; 2 when none is shaky or None and at least one is "panning"; 3 otherwise (camera not measured). Movement score: the mean of its halves' non-None movement (0 when none).

Repeatedly take the first remaining window as a stretch and grow it: pass over the remaining windows in start order and join each one of the same tier that overlaps or touches the stretch and whose movement differs from the first window's by at most the larger of CANDIDATE_ALIKE_SHARE x the larger of the two and MOVEMENT_SPREAD_FLOOR, as long as the joined stretch stays within CANDIDATE_MAX_SECONDS; repeat the pass until nothing joins. Then drop every remaining window that overlaps the stretch (touching is not overlapping). Stop at MAX_CANDIDATES. Ranks are 1, 2, 3 in the order taken.

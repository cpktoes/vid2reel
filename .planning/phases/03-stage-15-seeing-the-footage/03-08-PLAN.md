<objective>
Run the judge this stage was built for: the guide's Do-this on the Mac. Claude prepares a check folder of generated clips and the timing, then the user runs look on real footage, looks at the sheets in Finder, and runs the blind test on five clips (LOOK-07), logging every miss.

Plain English for the user: first Claude makes a small demo from test clips so you know what a sheet and look.json look like and how long look takes. Then it is your turn: you run look on one of your own clip sets, open the sheets, and ask yourself whether you could tell what happens from the sheet alone. Then the blind test: in a cleared Claude Code session, Claude sees only the sheets and look.json for one clip and must say what happens and which two seconds it would use. You compare with the clip. Four out of five is a pass.

Purpose: an automatic test can prove a still lands on a moving shape; only trying it proves a sheet gives a Claude that never saw the clip enough to choose well (the guide's "Why a blind test").
Output: the check folder, the timing, the user's blind-test result and the misses with their likely settings, in the SUMMARY.
</objective>

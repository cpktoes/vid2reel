<objective>
Put every remaining Stage 16 number into settings.py with its unit and a test that guards it, and make doctor and render know every FFmpeg piece the stage uses, so render refuses in one sentence on a computer that lacks one instead of failing half-way.

Plain English for the user: this plan does not change what a reel looks or sounds like. It writes down, in settings.py, every number the rest of the stage will use, each with a note saying what it measures: the 4 percent move, the touch-up caps, -14 LUFS, the fades, the third of a second the music takes to drop, the shortest reel (3 seconds), and so on. A test fails if any of them loses its note or if a number turns up in the block that nobody listed. Then doctor, the "is everything installed?" command, learns to check every piece of FFmpeg that render needs, and render checks the same list first, so on a phone whose FFmpeg lacks a piece you get one sentence naming it and nothing half-made.

Purpose: CLAUDE.md's rule that every number lives in settings.py (and CUT-04's and CUT-05's caps "in settings.py"), and the sandbox rule that doctor lists every filter render uses (CONTEXT: "doctor grows to list the filters render uses").
Output: the rest of the `# render:` block and the `# match:` block, tests/test_render_settings.py, doctor's muxer and demuxer lists, render's piece lists and refusal, and their tests.
</objective>

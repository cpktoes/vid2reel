<objective>
Give a shot its optional slow move, and build photos from their full-size picture.

Plain English for the user: a shot in the cut list can now have a slow push-in, pull-out or pan. Every move is the same small amount (4 percent of the frame, one setting), at one steady speed from the first picture of the shot to the last, so it is noticed only when you look for it. A pan zooms in by that same 4 percent so it always has room, then travels exactly that far, the way a camera turning looks. Photos are now always built from the big upright copy normalize saved, so a photo stays sharp in the reel whether it moves or not.

Purpose: CUT-04, and the photo part of CUT-03. This is the stage's "trickiest part" (CONTEXT): a move that stutters is a fault only an eye on a phone can judge, so the tests measure smoothness on a thin line at the full frame size.
Output: move windows in plan.py, the perspective move and the photo piece in video.py, new test clips in tests/media.py, tests/test_render_plan.py and tests/test_render_video.py.
</objective>

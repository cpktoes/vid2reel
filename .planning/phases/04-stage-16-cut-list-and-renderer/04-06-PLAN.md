<objective>
Build the reel's sound: each shot's own sound kept or off, music underneath from its start point with fades, the music dropped under kept sound, and the whole mix evened to one stated loudness.

Plain English for the user: a shot's own sound is now kept or switched off exactly as the cut list says. A music track plays underneath the whole reel from the second the list names (until the music library arrives in Stage 18 it is a plain low test tone), fading in over half a second and out over the last 1.5 seconds unless the list says otherwise. Under every shot whose own sound is kept (a laugh, someone talking) the music drops by the amount the list gives, starting a third of a second before and coming back a third of a second after. Finally the whole reel is measured and turned up or down to -14 LUFS, the level YouTube uses and the common target for short videos, so no reel is quieter than the last one in her feed; render says the figure it used.

Purpose: CUT-06, and the loudness figure in CUT-07's closing sentence (D-27). The music library and ducking that follows the real sound are Stage 18.
Output: the music, drop and loudness parts of v2r/render/sound.py, the gain in render(), tests/test_render_sound.py.
</objective>

<objective>
Finish the checker's rulebook: every rule the design settled is checked against the work folder and the music, each broken rule is one plain sentence, and the things worth saying but not worth refusing are notes.

Plain English for the user: `check` now knows every rule. It says when a shot runs past its clip, when the music would run out before the reel ends ("The music runs out at 14.0 seconds but the reel is 20.0 seconds long."), when a touch-up number is over its cap, when the cover points at a shot that does not exist, or when a reel is too short or too long to send back. It also says, without refusing, when a photo has no move, when there is no touch-up, when a reel has no sound at all, and when a sideways clip in a tall reel keeps only about a third of its picture, as the guide asks Claude to say.

Purpose: the rest of CUT-02 (D-10, D-15, D-17, D-21's note). With this plan, a list that `check` passes is one render can build exactly.
Output: the remaining rules and notes in v2r/render/check.py; the full sentence table in tests/test_cutlist_check.py.
</objective>

<objective>
Make the clips look like one shoot: `match` works out a small, capped correction per clip from look's measurements and writes it into the cut list as numbers, and render applies exactly those numbers and nothing else to colour.

Plain English for the user: look already measured how bright each clip is and how much it leans blue or red. `match` takes the middle of those (the median, so one odd clip does not drag the rest) and writes, for each clip, a nudge towards that middle: at most 8 steps of brightness either way (about a third of a stop), at most 4 steps of blue or red (a faint tint), and saturation between 0.92 and 1.08 (a hair). It never pushes towards plain grey, so a golden-hour shoot stays golden; the clips only come to agree with each other. The numbers sit in the cut list where you can read them, render applies exactly them, and running match again after halving a cap gives new numbers.

Purpose: CUT-05 (D-06, D-22 to D-25). The handbook decides when to run match (Stage 17); a list without a touch-up is built with its colour untouched.
Output: v2r/render/match.py and its sub-command, the touch-up filter in v2r/render/video.py, tests/test_match.py and new tests in tests/test_render_video.py.
</objective>

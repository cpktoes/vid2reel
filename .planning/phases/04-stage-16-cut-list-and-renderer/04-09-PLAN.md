<objective>
Finish the outputs and prove the whole renderer: the cover still beside the reel, the size limit and failure safety in every case, and golden renders of both approved examples showing that the same cut list always makes the same video.

Plain English for the user: render now also saves a cover still next to the reel: the moment the cut list names (a shot number and a second into it), or the first frame when it names none, which is what the platforms show by default. If a reel is too long to come back through the chat at full size, render makes it smaller and says so; if it cannot fit at all, check refuses before anything is built. Then the big test: your two example reels, the 20-second tall one and the 38-second wide one, are built from made-up clips with known colours and checked frame by frame, colour by colour, and built twice to prove the same list gives the same video.

Purpose: CUT-07 (the cover and the limit) and CUT-09 (golden renders, D-11). After this plan the renderer is complete for Stage 16.
Output: the cover in plan.py, proof.py and render(); a split-clip helper in tests/media.py; tests/test_render_golden.py.
</objective>

<objective>
Run the rehearsals the guide asks for: each of the five fixture sets edited by a fresh Claude Code session acting as the phone's Claude, with EDITOR.md and one account's profile; one set again with a different profile; then the five reels watched with her, scored, the worst one traced, and one thing changed at a time until she would post at least three of the five with small changes.

Plain English for the user: this is the real test of the handbook. You paste the prompt from 05-REHEARSAL.md into a fresh Claude Code window once per fixture set; each makes a reel by following EDITOR.md alone. Claude then checks the files it left and lines up the two cut lists made with different profiles, so you can see what changed. You watch everything on a phone, with her if you can, and she scores each reel. Claude cannot watch or listen, so what to look for is spelled out below.

Purpose: HAND-04 (rehearsals and the second profile) and HAND-05 (three of five she would post).
Output: five rehearsed reels and a profile pair in work/ (private), her scores in fixtures/reel-log.md (private), and a SUMMARY with counts and the handbook's unclear places only.
</objective>

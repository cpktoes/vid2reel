## Plain English for the user

A cut list can now say which recipe it was cut to, Lively or Calm. The shot lengths (Lively one and a half to three seconds, Calm three to five) move into settings.py, and a test checks they match the sentence in your handbook. If you ever change one, the test reminds you to change the other. When a shot is longer or shorter than its recipe, the checker now adds one plain note. It never refuses: a deliberate slow shot later in a reel still renders. A photo may run longer than the recipe, up to its four seconds. Older lists that name no recipe are read as Lively, and the checker says so. Nothing about how a reel looks or sounds changes.

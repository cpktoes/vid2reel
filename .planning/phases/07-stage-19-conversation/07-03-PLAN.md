Two more follow-ups. "Start with the one where the dog runs in" becomes a reorder: the shots play in a new order, but each shot is exactly the same stretch of video as before. "Drop that clip" takes out every shot of that clip, or just one shot. The cover still moves with its shot. Nothing here changes how a reel is rendered.

## Plain English for the user

Before you test on the phone, the Mac runs the same conversation itself on made-up clips. It makes a reel, then "shorter", "mute it" and "a wide version", and renders each one from the newest list. The test checks each reel's length to the frame: 5.5 seconds, then 4.5, still 4.5 when muted, still 4.5 when wide. It checks the muted reels are silent and the wide one really is wide. It also shows that making the wide version needs no new contact sheets and no new touch-up. The colour numbers already in the list carry over. You can't see that on made-up clips, so on the phone, check that the wide version's colours look as even as the tall one's. README.md gets a line for the new command.

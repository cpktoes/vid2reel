## Plain English for the user

Before the branch goes to GitHub, where anyone can read it, Claude runs every test, about 1,100 of them in a quarter of an hour. It checks that the only code changes are the new revise command, the recipe notes and their settings. Then it searches everything that would be uploaded for anything private: video or photo files, e-mail addresses, this Mac's paths, secrets and credit lines in commit messages. One search is yours. This stage's executors never open the fixtures folder, so they cannot know the private names. You run three short commands with your own list of words; Claude gives you them. Then you say whether to upload. Main is never touched.

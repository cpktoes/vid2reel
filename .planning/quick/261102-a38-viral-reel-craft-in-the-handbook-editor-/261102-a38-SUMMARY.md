# Quick 261102-a38: Viral reel craft in the handbook Summary

The handbook may now hold up to 1,500 words (it is 1,299), it teaches the phone's Claude four short-form craft rules, and the three profile drafts carry the matching taste lines for the account owner to confirm or strike.

## What changed, in plain English

- **The word cap.** The test that guards `EDITOR.md` now allows 1,500 words instead of 1,200. A handbook of exactly 1,500 words passes and 1,501 fails with the sentence "EDITOR.md has 1501 words; the cap is 1500. Cut something before adding." The test for that was renamed to match.
- **Four new handbook rules**, one sentence each, pasted word for word:
  1. The opener's first frame is the reel's thumbnail, so it should show the thing big and sharp with no hand or body sweeping in.
  2. The last shot should be one the opener could follow from, so a rewatch runs round like a loop.
  3. The first three shots are the shortest in the reel; a slow shot comes later.
  4. In a tall reel the caption and buttons cover the bottom fifth, so the thing the reel is about stays in the top two thirds.
- **Records.** The decision is written into the "Settled so far" sentence in `CLAUDE.md` and as one dated 2026-11-02 line at the end of `docs/decisions.md`. Neither names a person or an account. They also record that words on screen, speed and a voice-over wait for Stages 21 and 25.
- **Nothing else in the handbook moved.** Exactly three lines of `EDITOR.md` changed, every command line is as it was, and neither Mac-only option was added.

## The profile drafts (not in the repo)

These live in `fixtures/profiles/`, which is never committed. Every new or changed line ends in `[check]` so the account owner can confirm or strike it. Each draft is now 28 lines.

- **Renovation draft** (1 line marked before, 6 now): length of twelve to twenty seconds instead of about 20; a face in the first two seconds; keep the sound of the work itself (sanding, spraying, drilling, sawing); the finished result held still about a second; the brief answer on length.
- **Lifestyle draft** (5 marked before, 9 now): the same length change; a face in the first two seconds, with a showing still opening on the house or the view; keep a real sound that is the point (waves breaking, a door opening onto the view); the widest-view ending held about a second; the brief answer on length.
- **Shaping draft** (0 marked before, 4 now): the same length change; the opener stays on the board or the screen with a face following within two seconds; a one-second held ending on the finished board or the app's result; the brief answer on length. This draft already kept the tool's own sound ("Keep the sound on a shot where the tool is the subject"), so that line was left exactly as it is.
